## Works in any theme

Design with roles, never with colors. If it only looks right in the theme you built it in, it isn't finished.

## Keyboard first

Everything can be reached, read and undone without a mouse. Focus is always visible.

## Quiet chrome, loud content

The interface stays out of the way so a person's theme, wallpaper and work carry the color.

## On the grid

Shapes sit on the pixel grid. The stepped bands and the pixel unit are the brand's texture, not an effect laid on top.

## One good default

Pick the answer and ship it. Options are for what people genuinely disagree on, not for what we didn't decide.
