## What this is

A reference for anyone who makes something for Omarchy: a theme, a wallpaper, an app, a poster. It says what the brand is, how the desktop is put together, and which rules keep both working in whatever theme a person runs.

It's modelled on [Primer](https://primer.style/), GitHub's design system, with one difference that shapes everything: Omarchy is themed by its users, so this system names color roles and never declares colors.

## Status

Early. The foundations and the site itself are being built first; brand rules and desktop components follow. Pages marked **draft** are open to change, and items marked **soon** in the sidebar are planned.
