## The marks

Three marks, one family. Use the **wordmark** wherever there's room to say the name, the **icon** where there isn't — app icons, favicons, avatars — and the **lockup** when both should be seen together.

## Construction

The wordmark is drawn on a grid of {GRID.cols} by {GRID.rows} square pixels, and every edge sits on it. That grid is the brand's unit: clear space, the lockup gap and the [stepped bands](../graphic-elements/) are all counted in its pixels.

## Lockup

The icon is scaled to the body of the wordmark's "o", so the two share a centre line, and sits a quarter of its own height to the left. Don't build it by hand — take it from [Downloads](../downloads/) or the [Logo Foundry](../../logo/).

## Clear space

Keep a quarter of the mark's height clear on every side, rounded up to whole pixels: {MIN.clear} pixels around the wordmark and the lockup. Nothing else — text, edges, other logos — enters it. More room is always fine.

## Minimum size

On screens, give every grid pixel at least two screen pixels: the wordmark no narrower than {MIN.wordmark}px. At one screen pixel per grid pixel it still holds, but only just — that's the floor. The icon goes down to {MIN.icon}px, the size of a favicon.

## Color

The mark is one color or one stepped fill, never anything in between.

- **Flat**, in the theme's `foreground` on its `background`. Use it at small sizes, in interfaces, and anywhere print has one ink.
- **Stepped**, built from the theme's `accent` by the [band rule](../graphic-elements/#stepped-bands). Use it where the mark is the point of the page.

Because the stepped fill comes from the accent, the same rule holds in every theme — including ones that don't exist yet.
